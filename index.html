The store

Draping Kukatpally
since 1999

Maguva opened on Nizampet Road in 1999. More than twenty-five years later, reviewers still describe it the same way — “one of the oldest and famous cloth stores in Hyderabad.” Sarees are still unfolded on the counter, one by one, the way they have always been shown — because a drape should be seen in full before it is chosen.
